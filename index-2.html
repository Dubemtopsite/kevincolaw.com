About Dato’ Kevin Sathiaseelan @ Joshua Kevin

Dato’ Kevin Sathiaseelan @ Joshua Kevin is the Managing Partner of Messrs. Hui Doe & Sum Law Firm LLP. He practiced law for a decade at Messrs. Syed Alwi, Ng & Co., where he held office as a Partner before he established a firm of his own. During his tenure at Messrs. Syed Alwi, Ng & Co., Dato’ Joshua was the lawyer in-charge for Criminal, Civil and Banking Litigation alongside other renowned partners of the firm. In Messrs. Hui Doe & Sum Law Firm LLP., Dato’ Joshua continues to practice Criminal, Civil and Commercial Litigation actively. He also practices Intellectual Property Law, where many of the cases have been reported in the Law Journal.

Dato’ Joshua is a litigation lawyer renowned for his exceptional skills in navigating the intricate landscape of legal disputes. With an extensive background in litigation, he possesses a deep understanding of the various stages and complexities involved in resolving legal conflicts.

Legal Writing and Expertise

When it comes to legal writing, Dato’ Joshua possesses a keen ability to distill complex legal concepts into clear and persuasive arguments. Whether drafting pleadings, motions, or legal memoranda, he carefully crafts each document to present his client's position in the most compelling manner possible. Dato’ Joshua's expertise extends to a diverse range of litigation matters. He has successfully represented clients in civil litigation, encompassing contract disputes, property disputes, and tort claims. Additionally, he has a strong background in commercial litigation, handling complex business disputes, breach of contract cases, partnership conflicts, and minority oppression cases. His experience also spans regulatory matters, such as investigations and enforcement actions by government agencies.

Professional Development and Contributions

To stay at the forefront of his field, Dato’ Joshua remains committed to professional development and continuing education. He actively participates in legal seminars, workshops, and conferences to stay updated on the latest legal trends, precedents, and strategies. Dato’ Joshua is also a Criminal Procedure lecturer at Advance Tertiary College (ATC); Adjunct Professor at Taylor’s University Faculty of Law; and an External Advisor at Multimedia University Faculty of Law. He is the author of several law textbooks which includes Land Law, Laws of Hire-Purchase, Criminal Law, and Insurance Law. By staying informed and adapting to evolving legal landscapes, he ensures that his clients benefit from the most current and effective legal approaches.
